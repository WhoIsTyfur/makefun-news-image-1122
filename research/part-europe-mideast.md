### 1. COP31 climate talks in Antalya end without a deal [climate / diplomacy] CONFIRMED + OFFICIAL
- **When:** Sun 22 Nov, after 2 weeks of talks plus 2 days of overtime. The scheduled close was 20 Nov.
- **What happened:**
  - No cover decision or "Antalya Pact" was adopted. COP31 president Murat Kurum: "these decisions have not been adopted".
  - The EU formally objected to text that left out fossil fuels. Panama rejected the outcome and the Marshall Islands withheld support.
  - Some decisions were adopted: just transition, adaptation, and an Action Agenda.
  - The UN calls it the longest-running UN climate summit on record. Reuters says it is the first such collapse since 2009.
- **Guterres:** "There may be no consensus in the negotiating room. There is no ambiguity in the science." Outlets also report him as "deeply disappointed".
- **Next:** COP32 will be held in Addis Ababa.
